# Summary

Etcd is a sensitive storage boundary whose direct access differs from API-mediated resource access. Transport protection, at-rest encryption and caller permission serve different purposes, and keys and backups need their own controls.

Quorum replication and historical recovery solve different failure cases. Protect snapshot access, assess exposed credentials and coordinate cluster-record restoration with independent application data recovery.
